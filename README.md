# A City Through Time · Rome — web bundle

The files the web player and the museum pages of **Rome, 753 BCE to 2026** read: the model of every period, the plate, the
narration and music tracks, the subtitles, the pictures and the four museum pages (the Colosseum, the Theatre of Pompey and
its hall, Old St Peter's, New St Peter's). They are served from GitHub Pages so the portal can load them:
`https://alexliyu7352.github.io/city-through-time-rome/rome/`.

- Watch the film, walk the old cities and open the museum pages: https://cities.go5.si/rome/
- The city's text (config, script, sources in `FACTS.md`, credits in `CREDITS`) and the software:
  https://github.com/alexliyu7352/city-through-time/tree/main/cities/rome

`CREDITS` says otherwise: third-party pictures, data and recordings keep their own licences, and the narration voice is not
